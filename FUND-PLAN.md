## Fund Strategy

### Investment Thesis

SpaceX Starbase is creating the next great American boomtown. We're positioned at the intersection of:
- **Space industry growth** - Starship production and launches ramping up
- **Infrastructure development** - New roads, utilities, services needed
- **Population influx** - Engineers, contractors, support workers relocating
- **Land scarcity** - Limited developable land in the space corridor

We're buying land and developing properties **before** the institutional money arrives.

### Geographic Focus

**Primary Target Zone:**
- Brownsville to Boca Chica corridor
- Port Isabel
- South Padre Island (mainland side)
- Rio Hondo
- San Benito
- Los Fresnos

**Key Criteria:**
- Within 30-minute drive of Starbase
- On or near major transportation routes (Highway 4, Highway 48, Highway 100)
- Utilities available or feasible
- Zoning favorable for commercial/residential development

### Fund Structure (Initial Proposal)

- **Entity Type:** Texas Limited Partnership or LLC
- **Fund Size:** $5M - $25M initial raise
- **Minimum Investment:** $250K (accredited investors)
- **Management Fee:** 1.5% annually
- **Carried Interest:** 20% above 8% preferred return
- **Investment Period:** 5 years
- **Fund Life:** 7-10 years
- **Strategy:** Acquire, develop, operate, sell

### Investment Categories

1. **Raw Land** (30-40% of fund)
   - Large parcels in growth path
   - Hold for appreciation and future development
   - 3-7 year hold periods

2. **Development Projects** (40-50% of fund)
   - Workforce housing (apartments, townhomes)
   - Commercial properties (retail, office, flex space)
   - Mixed-use developments
   - 2-5 year projects

3. **Cash-Flowing Assets** (10-20% of fund)
   - Existing rental properties
   - Small commercial buildings
   - Immediate income to support fund operations

### Target Returns

- **IRR Target:** 18-25%
- **Cash-on-Cash:** 10-15% annual distributions
- **Equity Multiple:** 2.5x - 3.5x over fund life

```
Primary: Deep charcoal (#1a1a1a) / True black (#000000)
Secondary: Stark white (#ffffff)
Accent: Burnt orange (Texas) (#bf5700)
Accent 2: Deep space blue (#0a1929)
Highlight: Metallic silver/chrome effects
```

**Typography:**
- **Headers:** 72px-120px+ on desktop (MASSIVE)
- **Body:** 18-24px (highly readable)
- **Font families:** 
  - Display: Space Grotesk, Druk, or similar geometric sans

**Imagery:**
- Starship launches (hero imagery)
- Aerial views of South Texas landscape
- Construction/development progress shots
- Wide Texas horizons
- Minimal text overlays, let images speak

## Technical Implementation

### Recommended Stack

**Option 1: Next.js (Professional Grade)**
- Next.js 15 + React 19
- Tailwind CSS (easier to maintain than custom CSS)
- Vercel deployment
- Sanity or Contentful CMS (for portfolio updates)
- Image optimization built-in
- SEO-friendly
- Fast, scalable, maintainable

**Option 2: Static HTML/CSS (Simpler, but requires discipline)**
- Custom HTML5/CSS3
- Minimal JavaScript
- Cloudflare Pages or DigitalOcean
- Manually updated
- Faster initial build, harder to maintain

**Recommendation:** Next.js. This is a fund website representing millions of dollars. It needs to be flawless, fast, and easy to update as the fund grows.

### Key Features

- **Performance:** Sub-1-second load time
- **Mobile:** Fully responsive (many investors browse on mobile)
- **SEO:** Optimized for "starbase real estate", "south texas development", etc.
- **Security:** HTTPS, secure forms, privacy policy
- **Analytics:** Track visitor behavior, conversion paths
- **Legal Compliance:** SEC disclaimers, accredited investor requirements
