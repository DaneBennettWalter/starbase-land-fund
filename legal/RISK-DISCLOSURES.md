## 1. SPECULATIVE NATURE AND RISK OF LOSS

**Investing in the Fund is speculative and involves a high degree of risk.**

- **Loss of Principal:** Investors may lose some or all of their capital contribution. There is no assurance that investors will realize a return on their investment or that they will not lose their entire investment.
  
- **No Guarantee of Returns:** The Fund does not guarantee any specific rate of return, and there can be no assurance that the Fund will achieve its investment objectives.

- **Unsuitable for Essential Funds:** Prospective investors should not invest funds that they cannot afford to lose. The Fund is suitable only for sophisticated investors who can bear the economic risk of losing their entire investment.

- **Variable Returns:** Actual returns may vary materially from any estimates, projections, or targets provided. Investment results depend on numerous factors, many of which are beyond the Fund's control.

## 2. ILLIQUIDITY

**Investments in the Fund are highly illiquid.**

- **No Public Market:** There is no public market for interests in the Fund (the "Units"), and none is expected to develop.

- **Limited or No Redemption Rights:** Investors will have limited or no ability to redeem or withdraw their investment. Units are not redeemable at the option of the investor except in limited circumstances, if any, as specified in the Fund's operating documents.

- **Long Investment Horizon:** Investors should be prepared to hold their investment for an extended period, potentially for the entire life of the Fund, which may be ten years or longer.

- **Transfer Restrictions:** Units are subject to substantial restrictions on transferability. Any transfer of Units requires the prior written consent of the Fund's management and must comply with applicable securities laws.

- **Impact on Financial Planning:** The illiquid nature of this investment may significantly affect an investor's overall financial position and ability to access capital when needed.

## 3. REAL ESTATE MARKET RISKS

**Real estate investments are subject to various market and economic risks.**

- **Market Volatility:** Real estate values can fluctuate significantly based on national, regional, and local economic conditions, interest rate changes, unemployment rates, consumer spending, and other macroeconomic factors.

- **Supply and Demand:** Changes in the supply of or demand for competing properties in an area can materially affect property values and rental income.

- **Economic Downturns:** Economic recessions or downturns can lead to decreased property values, lower occupancy rates, reduced rental rates, and increased operating costs.

- **Interest Rate Risk:** Rising interest rates may increase the cost of borrowing, decrease property values, and negatively impact the Fund's ability to finance acquisitions or refinance existing debt.

- **Property-Specific Risks:** Individual properties may face risks including environmental issues, physical deterioration, title defects, zoning changes, or local market conditions that affect their value or income-generating potential.

## 4. LEVERAGE AND FINANCING RISKS

**The Fund may employ leverage, which magnifies both gains and losses.**

- **Amplified Losses:** Use of borrowed funds can substantially increase the risk of loss. If property values decline or rental income decreases, leveraged properties may experience disproportionate losses.

- **Debt Service Obligations:** The Fund will have fixed debt service obligations regardless of property performance. Failure to meet these obligations could result in default, foreclosure, and loss of the underlying property.

- **Refinancing Risk:** The Fund may need to refinance debt at maturity. There is no guarantee that refinancing will be available on favorable terms, or at all, which could force asset sales at disadvantageous times.

- **Loan Covenants:** Loan agreements may contain restrictive covenants that limit the Fund's operational flexibility and ability to respond to changing circumstances.

- **Reliance on Management:** Investors must rely entirely on the experience, judgment, and integrity of the Fund's management team for all investment decisions, property selection, acquisition, financing, operation, and disposition.

- **Competition:** The Fund faces competition from other real estate investors, funds, and institutions, which may reduce available investment opportunities or compress returns.

## 13. INVESTOR SUITABILITY AND ELIGIBILITY

**Investing in the Fund is limited to qualified investors who meet specific suitability requirements.**

### Accredited Investor Requirement

Investment in the Fund is generally limited to "accredited investors" as defined under Regulation D of the Securities Act of 1933. To qualify as an accredited investor, an individual must meet at least one of the following criteria:

- **Income Test:** Annual income exceeding $200,000 (or $300,000 with a spouse or spousal equivalent) in each of the two most recent years, with a reasonable expectation of reaching the same income level in the current year.

- **Net Worth Test:** Net worth exceeding $1,000,000, either individually or jointly with a spouse or spousal equivalent, excluding the value of the individual's primary residence.

- **Professional Credentials:** Holding in good standing one or more professional certifications or designations recognized by the SEC, such as Series 7, Series 65, or Series 82 licenses.

- **Entity Test:** Entities with total assets exceeding $5,000,000, or entities in which all equity owners are accredited investors.

- **Knowledgeable Employees:** Certain employees of the Fund who have professional knowledge regarding the Fund's investments.

### Qualified Purchaser Requirement (if applicable)

Certain offerings may require investors to be "qualified purchasers" as defined in the Investment Company Act of 1940, which generally requires:

- Individuals with at least $5,000,000 in investments.
- Family companies with at least $5,000,000 in investments.
- Entities (acting for their own account or on behalf of other qualified purchasers) with at least $25,000,000 in investments.

### Suitability Standards

In addition to meeting eligibility requirements, investors must:

- Have adequate net worth and income to afford the complete loss of their investment.
- Have sufficient liquidity to hold their investment for an indefinite period without need for access to capital.
- Have sufficient knowledge and experience in financial and business matters to evaluate the merits and risks of the investment.
- Understand and accept the illiquid nature of the investment and all associated risks.

### Responsibility for Self-Assessment

**Each prospective investor is responsible for determining whether they meet the applicable suitability and eligibility requirements.** The Fund and its representatives do not provide investment, legal, or tax advice and are not responsible for assessing an investor's suitability. Investors should consult their own advisors to make an independent determination regarding their eligibility and suitability.
